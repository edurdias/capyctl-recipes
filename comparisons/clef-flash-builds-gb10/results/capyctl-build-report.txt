# Clef checkpoint experiment

Agreement is against BF16, not gold-label correctness. Wilson intervals are descriptive question-level intervals; questions within requests can be correlated.

Sensitivity and layout-selection numbers are BF16-arithmetic simulations with explicit activation QDQ, not quantized-kernel measurements. Final agreement and timing below use the unmodified stock loader. Hooks were confined to an experiment launcher outside the serving environment.

Calibration: 18 independent deterministic synthetic decision requests; exact disjointness from the held-out set checked. No held-out row was used for calibration, scale tuning or layout selection. Diagnostic selection uses the recovered 41-request / 104-question cohort.

ModelOpt 0.47.0 supplies calibration and weight rounding. Own compressed-tensors export is authorized because the approved ModelOpt FP8 loader supports per-tensor, not per-channel weights. The published per-channel FP8 layout was round-tripped before new tensors were built. Static versus dynamic activations were selected by diagnostic simulation. Both joint and lexical heads stay byte-identical BF16.
