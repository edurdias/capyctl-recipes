Method

  • Every request is one POST /v1/systemone to the CapyCTL inference endpoint, with the deployment name as model. A decision generates no tokens: there is no time to first token and no tok/s.
  • Latency: client side, from the request written to the whole answer read, one request at a time. p50 and p95 are linear-interpolation percentiles over the runs at a point (100 by default, so p95 rests on about five requests). Per question is a request's latency over the questions it answered: amortized cost, not the latency of a single question.
  • State: deterministic filler text with a seed of its own for every request of the run, warm-ups included, so no request reuses another's prefix cache (the cache test's repeated block excepted). Its length is sized from two calibration requests: the growth of usage.input_tokens per filler sentence (4 characters per token when the engine reports no usage). Input tokens are usage.input_tokens per request: state, questions and the engine's template, so the same text can count differently on different models.
  • Questions: the first N of a fixed set of five (choice, noul, score, choice, noul). The results file's decisions.manifest holds them, their options and the serialization.
  • Cache: cold-prefix requests (each a new state) against one state repeated after its first sight.
  • Throughput: closed loop at a fixed number of streams; each stream sends its next request as soon as its last one returns, every request its own state. Requests and answered questions per second are the answered requests (questions) over the time from the first sent to the last finished; errors are the share of requests that failed.
  • Typed Decisions (LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions, test, 400 cases): each case's state and five questions in one request, as the dataset card asks. Accuracy: the answer's most probable option against the gold label (also reported counting a prediction tied for the gold's top as right, the public third-party convention). KL(gold ‖ prediction) with predictions clipped at 1e-9, and Brier as the sum over options of (p − gold)², against the soft gold; hard-label Brier against the one-hot label. Score MAE: |expected score − gold expected score| in levels.
  • Decision Index components (API-Bank, GPQA Diamond, Home appliances): rebuilt from pinned sources exactly as the decision-index kit builds them (byte-identical to its normalized rows), with its exclusions and subsets, sent unchanged, and scored on the answer's choice: accuracy, or case exact accuracy (every field right). Raw is score × coverage; skill = (raw − chance) / (1 − chance), clipped to [0, 1].
  • AG News and BoolQ: a fixed sample of each pinned set, one question per request. Accuracy and macro-F1 over the answered rows; a noul counts as yes at 0.5 or more. Brier, vector: mean of Σₖ(pₖ − yₖ)²; Brier, binary: mean of (p(yes) − y)², half the two-class vector value. NLL: mean −ln p(true class), p clipped at 1e-9.
  • ECE: top label (the most probable option and its probability), 10 equal-width confidence bins, the count-weighted mean of |accuracy − mean confidence|. The reliability charts plot each bin's accuracy at its mean confidence; the bin counts are in summary.md and data.csv.
  • Rows come from the Hugging Face datasets-server (checked against the sha256 pinned with the revision) or are rebuilt from pinned sources (checked against the kit's sha256). Results keep ids, hashes and answers, never the rows' text.
  • Peak memory: the highest value the memory command printed while the request, level or set ran.
  • Tables show the median with the min–max range across runs below it.
